Vinyl-safe paint The siding is vinyl. Light colors such as Alabaster are generally safe to use on vinyl. Darker body colors can warp or buckle vinyl unless they are mixed in a vinyl-safe formula, such as Sherwin-Williams VinylSafe. Regular Iron Ore (SW 7069) and Tricorn Black (SW 6258) are not VinylSafe body colors; do not use them on the siding itself. They are fine on wood or metal trim, doors, gutters, and railings.

Recommended prefab: a Michigan modular, not the Amazon foldable

The right product for this site is a Michigan state-code modular of about 1,200–1,530 square feet, 3 bedrooms and 2 baths, set on a permanent foundation. Modular homes are built to the same codes as a site-built house. Two local dealers that serve Grand Traverse County are Pine Grove Homes in Traverse City (231-947-6561) and Little Valley Homes in Cadillac (231-775-8102).

A concrete example is Pine Grove’s lot model PG #8: a Redman / Summit Michigan State Code Modular, 26′8″ × 52′, 1,387 square feet, 3 bed / 2 bath. The country-modern concept images use that scale, with an Alabaster body and Tricorn Black trim. Confirm a current package price with the dealer; a home-only package in this size typically runs about $160,000–$270,000 depending on finishes.

Amazon does sell prefab structures, including a two-level foldable house listed at $42,000 (ASIN B0HPLG8CF7). That listing’s own floor area is about 409 square feet — not 1,200–1,500 — and it does not state HUD or Michigan modular approval. Shipping copy on the listing describes export to Latin America. It is unlikely to be permitted as a dwelling in Whitewater Township and is not recommended here. Other Amazon “1,200–5,000 sf villa” prices could not be verified, and at least one steel “modular house” at about $20,000 is a framing kit, not a finished home.

A HUD-code manufactured home can be cheaper, but many models in this size band sit under 1,200 square feet and use a different code, which can affect financing and resale. Use that path only if the township and a lender are comfortable with it.
