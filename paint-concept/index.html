A paint and cottage concept for 10746 E Deal Road. On the main and upper levels, walls are Sherwin-Williams Shoji White, and trim, doors, railings, and wall paneling are Alabaster — except the main living room, which stays in its original darker colors for a piano night-club lounge feel. Outside is a country-modern scheme: Alabaster siding, Tricorn Black trim, and Iron Ore railings, with the existing 2019 roof kept. A 2 bedroom, 2 bath modular cottage of about 1,040 square feet is shown on the 4.67-acre east land (parcel 13-002-018-30), northeast of the house and the front barn — not on the 0.46-acre strip directly north of the house. It sits off the electric-line corridor, with its long axis running north–south so the porch faces the lake, a two-car parking pad on the west side, its own driveway, a walking trail to the main house, and the front barn and existing trees helping to screen it from parts of the house. Vinyl-safe paint The siding is vinyl. Light colors such as Alabaster are generally safe to use on vinyl. Darker body colors can warp or buckle vinyl unless they are mixed in a vinyl-safe formula, such as Sherwin-Williams VinylSafe. Regular Iron Ore (SW 7069) and Tricorn Black (SW 6258) are not VinylSafe body colors; do not use them on the siding itself. They are fine on wood or metal trim, doors, gutters, and railings.

Cottage on the east land

A 2 bedroom, 2 bath Michigan modular cottage of about 1,040 square feet is shown on the east land, one of the three existing parcels. It has a kitchen, a living room, a utility / laundry room, and a two-car parking pad outside.

It is not on the small parcel directly north of the house. That strip is parcel 13-002-018-01, 0.46 acre, immediately north of the house parcel and only about 100 by 200 feet. The cottage is on the larger east land, parcel 13-002-018-30, 4.67 acres, which runs as a long north–south lot along the east side of the house. Both the recommended pad (F) and the farther-north alternate (N) are on that east land. Pad F is about 300 feet northeast of the house and about 220 feet northeast of the front barn. Pad N is farther north on the same parcel, in denser pines; it is not on the 0.46-acre strip either.

The recommended placement, pad F, is northeast of the house and the front (north) barn, toward Elk Lake and well off the electric-line corridor that crosses the south field. An earlier concept sat just southeast of the south (rear) barn; that open field is the cleared power-line corridor, so that pad is not used. Placement was re-checked against a fresh Grand Traverse County leaf-off ortho, the county parcel lines, and the listing photos. Buildings and the driveway were re-digitized from that ortho: the earlier site-plan point for the front barn was about 39 feet too far northeast, which made the cottage look closer to that barn than it is. The cottage’s long axis runs north–south so the catalog porch on the living-room end faces north / north-northwest toward Elk Lake. Bedrooms face south, toward the front barn. The front door and the two-car parking pad face west, toward a new gravel driveway spur of about 190 feet (about a 5% grade) and the front barn. A walking trail, kept off the driveway, runs from the east deck through the trees to the cottage. The front barn and existing trees help screen the cottage from parts of the main house; bare-earth line-of-sight from the decks is still open, so keep and add plantings. These drawings are concepts, not a survey, and permits would be required. A full topo and siting review of all three parcels is on this site.

Recommended modular: Rochester American R31-26

The right product for this site is a Michigan state-code modular of about 900–1,200 square feet, 2 bedrooms and 2 baths, set on a permanent foundation. Modular homes are built to the same codes as a site-built house. Two local dealers that serve Grand Traverse County are Pine Grove Homes in Traverse City (231-947-6561) and Little Valley Homes in Cadillac (231-775-8102). Pine Grove already sells Rochester 26-foot ranch modulars.

The plan used for the footprint and concept views is the Rochester Homes American R31-26: 26′2″ × 40′0″, 1,040 square feet, 2 bed / 2 bath, with a kitchen, living room, and a utility / laundry room. An optional 8-foot covered porch sits on the living-room short end — that is why the cottage is turned north–south, so the porch faces the lake. The front door is on a long wall; here that door and the parking pad face west. Confirm a current package price with the dealer; a home-only package in this size typically runs about $120,000–$200,000 depending on finishes. A slightly larger alternate is the Jefferson R34-26 (26′2″ × 44′, 1,144 sf, 2 bed / 2 bath). Little Valley’s Cozy Cabin, a Ritz-Craft modular, is 2 bed / 2 bath and 1,297 square feet, a bit over this size band. Other dealer pages for the R31-26 include Modular Homes of Michigan and Thorne’s Homes.

Amazon does sell prefab structures, including a two-level foldable house listed at $42,000 (ASIN B0HPLG8CF7). That listing’s own floor area is about 409 square feet, and it does not state HUD or Michigan modular approval. Shipping copy on the listing describes export to Latin America. It is unlikely to be permitted as a dwelling in Whitewater Township and is not recommended here. Other Amazon “1,200–5,000 sf villa” prices could not be verified, and at least one steel “modular house” at about $20,000 is a framing kit, not a finished home.

A HUD-code manufactured home can be cheaper, but many models in this size band use a different code, which can affect financing and resale. Use that path only if the township and a lender are comfortable with it.

Topo and siting

A full topographic review of all three parcels, using USGS 3DEP 1-meter lidar, shaped the cottage placement. The maps below show 2-foot contours, slope, bare-earth viewsheds toward Elk Lake from ground height and from a second-floor or deck height, and line-of-sight profiles between the main house and each candidate pad. They also mark the approximate electric-line corridor (digitized from aerials, not a surveyed easement), existing drives and buildings, driveway grades, and septic-suitable ground (gentle slope, off the corridor, away from the well). These maps are also on the Topo & siting page. They are not a survey. Confirm the recorded easement width with title or the utility, and confirm well, septic, and the real lake view on the ground, preferably leaf-off.

Recommended pad: F, northeast of the front (north) barn on the east land, about 792 ft elevation (17 ft below the house), mean slope about 10%, about 450 ft from the power line. A 190-foot driveway spur can stay around 5% grade. Bare-earth rays toward Elk Lake are mostly clear at both 6 ft and 16 ft eye height; trees will screen or frame the real view. Pad N, further north, is the easiest alternate (flatter, shorter drive) but sits 39 ft below the house and more directly in the north lake-view cone. Pads H and S, east and south of the house, are closer and more exposed, and S is tight to the corridor. Pad A, in the corridor, is not used.
