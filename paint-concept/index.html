Vinyl-safe paint The siding is vinyl. Light colors such as Alabaster are generally safe to use on vinyl. Darker body colors can warp or buckle vinyl unless they are mixed in a vinyl-safe formula, such as Sherwin-Williams VinylSafe. Regular Iron Ore (SW 7069) and Tricorn Black (SW 6258) are not VinylSafe body colors; do not use them on the siding itself. They are fine on wood or metal trim, doors, gutters, and railings.
