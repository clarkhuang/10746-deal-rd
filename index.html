The plan

Main-floor nightclub lounge + primary suite. Upstairs offices and full-room closet. Lower apartment-style retreat. Front barn for vehicles; rear barn for CrossFit and recovery. Optional outdoor plunge on the East Deck (east / back, toward Elk Lake), with a 4-person wood-fired sauna on a pier just east of it.

Main

Patio slider → dining/sunroom → kitchen with island → vaulted living. Living’s east glass looks over the East Deck toward Elk Lake but has NO exterior deck door; reach the East Deck from outside via the dining slider, north stone patio, and deck wrap. The east–west hall runs from the living room past the primary suite (south of living), pantry, bifold closet, laundry/sink and stairs. Heading west, the half bath is the last door on the right, just before a T at the garage door: left (south) goes out to the inset south courtyard, right (north) goes toward the front. Garage overhead doors face west, opposite the East Deck. There is no separate mudroom. Primary is not entered directly from living.

Upper

One long hall runs from the stair landing, with a rail over the living room on its east side (toward the East Deck). Off the west side of the hall, in one row from north to south: north room (Jenny’s office), middle room (Jenny’s closet), full bath (straight ahead of the stairs) and south room (Clark’s office). North and south rooms are about the same size and have end-wall doors. Which listing bedroom is which is unverified.

Lower

Stairs land at tiled hall: right through a wide opening to living and covered-patio glass exit; left to bedroom corridor. Bath has hall and bedroom-1 access only. Bedroom 2 is farther along; kitchenette is at the corridor end, with a side-yard exit and mechanical room off it. No direct living-to-kitchen opening is inferred.
